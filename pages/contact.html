FAQ

Common Questions.

How do I know my donation reaches the right people?

We publish quarterly impact reports and provide real-time tracking for all major donations. 90% of funds go directly to projects.

Can I volunteer remotely?

Yes! We have remote volunteer roles in content creation, translation, data analysis, and fundraising. Apply through our volunteer portal.

Do you accept corporate partnerships?

Absolutely. We have a dedicated corporate partnerships team. Email us at partnerships@hopeharmony.org to get started.
