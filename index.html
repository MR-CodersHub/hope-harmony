Who We Are

Driven by
Purpose.

For 20 years, Hope & Harmony has been at the forefront of humanitarian action — not just providing aid, but building lasting systems of change.

We believe that every human being deserves dignity, opportunity, and a future. Our programs are designed with communities, not just for them — ensuring that every initiative is culturally grounded, locally led, and globally supported.

20 Years of Impact

Our Milestones.

2005

Founded

Started with a mission to bring clean water to 10 villages in Kenya.

2010

Global Expansion

Expanded to 25 countries, reaching 500,000 people with education programs.

2018

Climate Action

Launched the Green Earth Initiative, planting 1 million trees in Year 1.

2025

2M Milestone

Crossed 2 million lives impacted across 50 countries. The mission continues.
